# Ethical Guard — Intellectual Property and Human Rights Policy

Owner contact: radosavleviciervin8@gmail.com

Verify ownership and licences before publishing or selling software and other intellectual property. Preserve original code, revision history and attribution. Require owner approval before changes, publication, licensing, transfers or financial transactions.

Document suspected unauthorised copying with relevant dates, sources and evidence. Distinguish allegations from established findings. Protect privacy, provide human review and appeal, and avoid automatic accusations, penalties or confiscation.

Apply international human rights law and ethics, including dignity, non-discrimination, fair process, proportionality and applicable intellectual-property and data-protection law. NDA provisions and legal remedies require qualified review.

This is a policy proposal, not a finding of infringement.
